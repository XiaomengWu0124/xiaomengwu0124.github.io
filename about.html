About

I'm interested in art history, visual culture, and the experience of looking carefully. I spend a lot of time in museums and write about what I find there—how light, labels, and exhibition design quietly shape attention, interpretation, and memory.

I also love traveling. Being an outsider—moving through unfamiliar streets, languages, and customs—often sharpens my sense of the inner landscape: what I notice, what I miss, and what I carry with me.

Also a Manchester City supporter. Come on City!

This site is a home for my photography, museum notes, and art commentary. You can reach me at xmwu0124@gmail.com.
